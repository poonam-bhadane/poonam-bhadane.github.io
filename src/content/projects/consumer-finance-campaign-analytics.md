## The problem

Nordbank Consumer Finance is a fictional arm of a global bank offering loans, card-based financing and buy-now-pay-later through retail, e-commerce and mobility partners. Management cannot see a unified view of the customer across channels, so campaigns are untargeted and their performance is hard to measure.

## What the project covers

The project is written as a set of documents that work through the problem the way an analyst would:

- **Data model** and data dictionary for one unified customer database
- **Segmentation and journey analysis**: value, behavioural and lifecycle segments, funnels and drop-off
- **KPI dictionary** and campaign performance reporting: reach, click-through, conversion, approval rate, funded volume and ROI
- **Data quality framework** and requirements: completeness, validity, uniqueness and consent checks
- **Consent management** design: a purpose-based consent ledger with withdrawal and right to be forgotten, shaped around GDPR
- **Audience delivery**: consent-gated exports of segments for campaign managers

## Be clear about what this is

This is a design and documentation project, and all data in it is synthetic.
