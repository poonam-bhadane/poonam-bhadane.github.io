## The idea

A customer data platform is easy to describe and hard to demonstrate without real systems. I do not have access to a production CDP, so I built a prototype that shows the same thinking.

## What I wanted to show

How does a marketer's request, such as "customers who looked at premium dog food and haven't bought", become an audience that actually reaches people? The answer touches several layers: the data being collected, the rules that decide who qualifies, the stores that remember who is in the audience, and the tools that send them a message.

## What I learned

- Some audiences must react in seconds, and some can wait for a nightly run. That choice drives the whole design.
- Buying versus building is a decision about each capability, not about the platform as a whole.
- A good audience builder produces a clear, checkable definition. It should not hide the logic from the person using it.
